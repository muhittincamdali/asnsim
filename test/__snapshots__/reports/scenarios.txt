billing-retry-expired
  A renewal fails without a grace period and the 60-day billing retry ends without payment.
  SUBSCRIBED/INITIAL_BUY → DID_FAIL_TO_RENEW → EXPIRED/BILLING_RETRY

billing-retry-grace-recovery
  A renewal fails, Billing Grace Period runs out, then billing retry recovers the subscription.
  SUBSCRIBED/INITIAL_BUY → DID_RENEW → DID_FAIL_TO_RENEW/GRACE_PERIOD → GRACE_PERIOD_EXPIRED →
  DID_RENEW/BILLING_RECOVERY

cancel-expire-winback
  The customer turns off auto-renew, the subscription expires, and a win-back offer brings them
  back.
  SUBSCRIBED/INITIAL_BUY → DID_CHANGE_RENEWAL_STATUS/AUTO_RENEW_DISABLED → EXPIRED/VOLUNTARY →
  SUBSCRIBED/RESUBSCRIBE

consumable-refund-dispute
  Coins bought, a refund request with a consumption request, the refund, then the refund reversed
  after a dispute.
  ONE_TIME_CHARGE → CONSUMPTION_REQUEST → REFUND → REFUND_REVERSED

family-sharing-revoke
  A family member gets the subscription through Family Sharing, then loses it.
  SUBSCRIBED/INITIAL_BUY → REVOKE

price-increase-accepted
  A price increase that needs consent, the customer consents, and the next renewal charges the new
  price.
  SUBSCRIBED/INITIAL_BUY → PRICE_INCREASE/PENDING → PRICE_INCREASE/ACCEPTED → DID_RENEW

price-increase-declined
  A price increase that needs consent goes unanswered and the subscription expires at renewal.
  SUBSCRIBED/INITIAL_BUY → PRICE_INCREASE/PENDING → EXPIRED/PRICE_INCREASE

trial-renew-refund
  A one-week free trial, its first paid renewal, then a refund of that renewal.
  SUBSCRIBED/INITIAL_BUY → DID_RENEW → REFUND

upgrade-downgrade
  Basic to Premium at once, then a downgrade back to Basic that waits for the next renewal.
  SUBSCRIBED/INITIAL_BUY → DID_CHANGE_RENEWAL_PREF/UPGRADE → DID_CHANGE_RENEWAL_PREF/DOWNGRADE →
  DID_RENEW
